Ten years ago, if you told me that I would be learning how to code in every free moment I had as a father of three, I would have a hard time believing it. Although, I'd grant it wouldn't be out of the realm of possibility. In truth, I probably would feel a bit heartbroken, because that would mean my first passion for media/film didn't pan out… Much sweat and effort went into that path and trying to make it work. I'd tell that younger self: "I don't know about that! There's still a lot of life ahead, and opportunities may present in the future." Then the younger self would look down at the three little children and see that future self is having a grand old time… That there's nothing to be bummed about—a family and a future is ahead…

This is a record of my journey learning programming (for now) by myself, with the little help from an "AI tutor" I've trained. I'm blown away this is possible. Welcome along!
